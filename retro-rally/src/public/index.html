      HOW TO PLAY

      Goal: complete the chosen number of laps as fast as you can. Your best lap goes to the leaderboard of the chosen track (VS BOTS mode).

      Checkpoints: every track has sectors marked with translucent lines across the road. A line disappears once you cross it, and all lines come back on each new lap. Pass them strictly in order — a missed sector keeps blinking red, and the lap does not count.

      Bridge: at the loop the road crosses over itself — the exit flies over the entry on a bridge.

      Grass slows you down, and tire posts bounce the car — stay on the asphalt.
